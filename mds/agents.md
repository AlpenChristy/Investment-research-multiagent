Financial Analyst — this is your data-heavy agent. It takes raw market data (revenue, margins, P/E, debt, cash flow) and produces a structured score. Its job is purely quantitative. Feed it Yahoo Finance / Finnhub data and prompt it to reason like a CFA analyst. Key output fields: summary, strengths[], weaknesses[], metrics{}, score.
News/Sentiment — the most "alive" agent because its output changes daily. It calls NewsAPI, optionally Reddit, and reasons about the tone of recent coverage. Its output is a sentiment enum (bullish / neutral / bearish) plus a confidence float. Confidence is what makes it interesting — 0.55 means uncertain, 0.92 means clear signal.
Risk Analyst — a pessimist by design. Prompt it to look for macro risks, regulatory threats, valuation excess, and competitive pressure. Crucially, give it a risk_level field (low / medium / high) that the synthesizer can weight. This is the agent most likely to surface things the bull misses.
Bull Analyst — receives the financial and news outputs, then makes the strongest possible case for buying. The key design choice: it sees the other agents' data but is instructed to argue for the position, not evaluate it neutrally. Give it catalysts[] (specific upcoming events that could drive price up) and an upside_target.
Bear Analyst — mirror of Bull. Same inputs, opposite mandate. The debate between Bull and Bear is your "wow factor" — the synthesizer then acts as a moderator. Both agents should have a conviction field (0–1) so the synthesizer can weight them when they conflict.
